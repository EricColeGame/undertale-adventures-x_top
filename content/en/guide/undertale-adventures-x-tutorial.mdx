## What Is Undertale Adventures X?

Undertale Adventures X is a free Roblox tower defense experience from Side Productions, the verified Roblox community that owns the game page. You defend a base against incoming monster waves using Undertale-inspired units, then level those units and strengthen them with traits between matches. This Undertale Adventures X tutorial walks through the opening loop, the four combat jobs your lineup needs, how trait rerolls actually work, and where codes and side systems fit into your progression.

If you are brand new, the important thing to understand is that this Undertale Adventures X tutorial is not about memorizing a single "best" unit. It is about spending a small opening purse wisely, upgrading with money earned from kills, and only gambling on traits once you can read the percentages on your own screen.

## Undertale Adventures X Tutorial: Surviving Your First Match

A match starts with the units you already own and a limited opening purse. The general shape of a first clear looks like this:

| Step | Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Place one unit that can actually hit the lane | An early placement that deals damage starts generating income |
| 2 | Bank kill money before upgrading | Upgrades scale better once you have a damage source already on the map |
| 3 | Add coverage for bosses and crowds | A single-role lineup stalls out around the mid waves |
| 4 | Upgrade the unit doing the most work | Concentrated investment beats spreading gold thin |
| 5 | Read the wave pattern before the final stretch | Knowing what is coming lets you place rather than panic |
| 6 | Finish the clear, then leave the match | Progression happens between matches, not during them |

Most community lineups repeat the same advice: get something on the board fast, then let defeated enemies pay for the next step. The opening purse is intentionally tight, so your first placement decision matters more than any later one.

### Between matches is where you actually grow

Once a clear is stable, the loop shifts. You summon or earn your next role, level the unit you plan to keep, and roll traits only after the trait screen shows you the percent. Codes, when they still work, go into the Codes button on the right side of the screen.

That order is not arbitrary. Leveling a unit raises a floor you can count on. Rolling traits is a gamble with a fixed rate table. Do the guaranteed upgrade first, then spend on the gamble.

## The Four Combat Jobs Every Lineup Needs

Player experience consistently points to four jobs rather than four specific units. Fill each job and you can swap in substitutes as your roster grows.

| Job | What it does | What happens if you skip it |
|---|---|---|
| Area damage | Clears groups of weaker enemies | Waves pile up faster than you can kill them |
| Boss damage | Bursts down single high-health targets | You stall on boss waves and lose the base |
| Front line | Holds a choke point and buys time | Enemies reach your damage units and end the run |
| Crowd control | Slows, stalls, or repositions enemies | Fast waves slip past your coverage gaps |

### Building around substitutes

Early on, you will not have a perfect unit for each job. That is fine. The goal is coverage, not perfection — a slightly weaker area-damage unit still does the job that an empty slot does not. As you unlock more Undertale-inspired units, upgrade the one filling your weakest role rather than chasing a new summon.

## Traits Explained: Read the Percent Before You Reroll

Traits are the long-term power layer. A reroll assigns one of the twelve listed traits, and the outcome is weighted by a rate table. Community-reported odds screens from the game's earlier updates showed rates as low as 15% for a specific result like Royal Guard Soul, which tells you something important: rerolls are a resource, not a slot machine you pull for fun.

| Trait concept | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Reroll | Assigns one of twelve traits; the old result is replaced |
| Rate | Each trait has a listed chance, visible on the trait screen |
| Buff traits | Change how a unit performs, not just its raw numbers |
| Chase traits | Low-rate, high-impact results worth saving rerolls for |

A November 29, 2024 community video described the Perseverance buff that the published rate table still shows. Whether current numbers match that table is something you should verify yourself — open your own Traits menu and read the percentage before you spend a large stack of rerolls.

### When to reroll

| Situation | Reroll? |
|---|---|
| You just got the unit and have not leveled it | No — level first |
| You have a handful of rerolls and one main carry | Yes, but only on that carry |
| You are rerolling a unit you rarely field | No |
| The trait screen shows a rate you have not read | No — read it first |

## Codes, Raids, the AFK Chamber, and the Underpass

Codes are the fastest early boost, but they expire. Community reports list 2KCCU! and FREESTUFF! as a working pair in earlier periods, with older strings archived as dead. Always type a code into the Codes button on the right side of the screen and check whether it was accepted before assuming it worked.

Beyond codes, three side systems open up once your clears are stable:

- **Raids tokens** — a currency tied to raid content that you accumulate as you play.
- **AFK Chamber** — passive progress while you are away from the keyboard.

## Beginner Mistakes and a Progression Checklist

Most early losses come from a short list of habits. Here is how to avoid them.

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Spending the whole opening purse on one expensive unit | Place something cheap that hits, then upgrade |
| Ignoring bosses until they reach the base | Keep one single-target damage unit ready |
| Rerolling traits on an unleveled unit | Level first, trait second |
| Copying a tier list without checking your roster | Build for role coverage, not for names |
| Typing codes without checking if they were accepted | Confirm the reward before moving on |
| Never touching Raids, AFK, or the Underpass | Use them once clears are stable |

### A simple weekly routine

1. Clear one defense to keep your income and habits sharp.
2. Redeem any code you have not tried yet, and note the result.
3. Level the unit that carried your last clear.
4. Roll traits on that same unit only.
5. Bank one raid or Underpass session for later power spikes.

This Undertale Adventures X tutorial intentionally puts leveling ahead of trait rolling every time, because that is the order that produces steady progress instead of a reroll stack burned on a unit you will replace.
