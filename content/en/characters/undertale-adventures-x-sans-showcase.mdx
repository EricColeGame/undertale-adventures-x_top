## What a Complete Undertale Adventures X Sans Showcase Should Cover

A "showcase" in tower defense communities usually means a short clip or post that demonstrates a unit doing its job. The problem is that most showcases only show the flashy part — the big damage number, the clutch save — and skip everything a viewer actually needs to replicate it.

A genuinely useful showcase documents the setup, not just the payoff. Here is the checklist worth following.

| Showcase Element | What to Record | Why Viewers Care |
|---|---|---|
| Damage profile | How Sans performs against single targets vs. groups | Tells you whether he is a boss answer or a wave-clearer |
| Placement | Exact spot on the map and why it was chosen | Placement is often the difference between good and useless |
| Upgrade path | Order in which levels were purchased | Shows the efficient way to spend early cash |
| Trait rolls | Which traits were equipped and how many rolls it took | Traits are the biggest power swing in the game |
| Team context | Which other units were on the field | A unit's performance depends heavily on its support |

### Damage Profile and Target Selection

Record how Sans behaves against a single tanky target versus a dense wave. That single comparison tells a viewer more about his role than any tier list label. If he shreds bosses but struggles to keep up with swarms, you have just explained why he needs crowd control beside him.

### Placement, Range, and Map Awareness

Placement is the most underrated part of any showcase. A unit dropped in the wrong lane looks weak; the same unit in the right spot looks broken. Show the map, mark the tile, and explain the reasoning — corner coverage, chokepoint overlap, or protecting a high-traffic path.

### Upgrade Path and Trait Rolls

Show your upgrade order on screen. New players routinely dump currency into the wrong upgrade tier early and then wonder why their Sans underperforms. Pair that with the trait rolls you used, and you have given them a complete, repeatable blueprint.

## Traits, Leveling, and How to Build Sans

Two systems carry most of your power in Undertale Adventures X: leveling and traits. Leveling raises a unit's baseline performance and is the more predictable of the two. Traits are rolled and rerolled, which means they are also where most players waste their resources.

| Priority | Trait Focus | What It Improves | When to Chase It |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Damage output | Raw effectiveness per hit | As soon as you commit to Sans long-term |
| 2 | Attack speed or cooldown | How often he contributes | After a solid damage roll is locked in |
| 3 | Range | Map coverage and placement freedom | When your favorite spots leave gaps |
| 4 | Economy or token traits | Long-term farming efficiency | Once your team can clear consistently |
| 5 | Situational utility | Niche stage-specific value | Last, and only if you have spare rolls |

Trait pools shift as the developer patches the game, so always check the current in-game list before spending. The general rule holds regardless of the exact names: lock in a damage-focused roll first, then layer speed or range on top.

Do not reroll endlessly on a unit you have not leveled yet. A maxed unit with a mediocre trait usually outperforms a low-level unit with a perfect one, because leveling raises the baseline that traits multiply.

## Team Synergy, Progression, and Common Mistakes

### Build Around Roles, Not Favorites

The most common roster mistake is stacking four damage units and wondering why the base keeps falling. Use the core five as a framework — Sans and Asriel for damage, Mettaton NEO for boss deletion, Undyne the Undying for frontline pressure, and Papyrus for control — then adjust based on the stage.

### Fund the Build With Raids and the AFK Chamber

Raids mode is the primary source of Tokens, the currency that drives roster progression. Player experience suggests the +50% Tokens gamepass pays for itself quickly if you farm Raids regularly, since it directly boosts that mode's yield. The AFK Chamber adds passive income while you are away, which compounds nicely with active farming sessions.

| Stage of Progression | Goal | Suggested Action |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Establish a functional team | Level your starter units and clear basic stages |
| Early-mid | Unlock consistent Raids clears | Prioritize a balanced role spread |
| Mid | Fund trait rolls | Farm Raids, use the AFK Chamber between sessions |
| Late | Optimize the core five | Reroll traits on Sans and your boss killer |
| Endgame | Push harder content | Fine-tune placement and support units |

### Mistakes That Ruin a Showcase

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Showing only the win screen | Viewers cannot replicate the result | Record placement and upgrade order |
| Ignoring team context | Suggests Sans works alone | Show the full lineup |
| Skipping trait details | Hides the biggest power variable | State the rolls used |
| Chasing numbers over roles | Encourages a broken roster | Explain the role Sans fills |
| Over-rolling early | Wastes Tokens on an unleveled unit | Level first, then roll |
