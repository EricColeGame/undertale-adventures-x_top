## Kit Breakdown: Moves, Blood Typing, and Critical Hits

The short kit is the most misunderstood part of this unit. Two moves sounds thin next to units that carry three or five, but each of his moves is built for area pressure rather than single-target poking.

| Move | What it does | Best used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bone Zone** | Ground-based bone strike that controls a patch of the lane | Opening a wave and softening grouped enemies |
| **Trident Strike** | Summons surrounding spears that crash down as heavy tridents | Bursting down what survived the first hit |

### Bone Zone as the opener

His first move plants a ground-based bone zone. In showcase testing it functions as the setup hit, tagging enemies before the heavier follow-up lands. Because it is area-focused, it works best against tightly packed waves rather than fast single targets.

### Trident Strike as the finisher

The second move summons spears around the target area and drops them as heavy tridents. This is where the physical critical strike scaling does its work. With a 10% base critical rate bonus baked in, the damage spread becomes noticeably wider once traits and support passives enter the picture.

### The passive, and the Blood typing question

Corridor Dust Sans carries a stacking +10% damage increase that, per community testing, has no observed cap. Long waves favor him enormously. There is also a well-known community observation that a Blood-typed unit does not apply standard bleed damage — showcase creators have flagged this as unexpected. In practice, his value comes from sustained critical hits rather than damage-over-time application.

## Team Synergy and Leader Skill Activation

This is where Undertale Adventures X Corridor Dust Sans either clicks or falls flat. His leader skill does not activate on its own. You must deploy another unit from the same category — Dust units being the obvious fit — for the effect to trigger at all.

| Teammate | Role in the comp | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Snowdin Dust** | Category trigger | Satisfies the Dust requirement while adding supplemental attack range |
| **Papyrus** | Stat support | Community testing observed baseline stats climbing by up to 15% through passive interaction |
| **Toriel (Fire)** | Element pairing | His passives complement Fire-type units |
| **Judge Sans** | DoT partner | Pairs well in multi-unit loadouts built around damage-over-time traits |
| **Unsettling Presence Fear trait** | Trait synergy | Reported to boost damage-over-time by 10% |

A few practical rules follow from that table:

- **Deploy the trigger unit early.** If Snowdin Dust or another Dust-category unit goes down late, you lose the leader skill window during the waves that matter most.
- **Do not treat Papyrus as optional if you have him.** A passive-driven bump toward a 15% stat increase is meaningful on a unit whose damage already scales over time.
- **Trait rerolling is inventory-dependent.** Damage-over-time traits like Unsettling Presence Fear are worth chasing only if you already run DoT units alongside him.
- **Range matters more than raw placement.** Because Snowdin Dust adds coverage, positioning the pair so both lanes are covered beats stacking them on one choke point.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many moves does Undertale Adventures X Corridor Dust Sans have?

Community showcase testing shows two distinct attack moves: a bone zone ground strike and an overhead trident drop. He does not carry a third or fourth ability, which is why his upgrade path feels shorter than many comparable units.

### How do you activate the Corridor Dust Sans leader skill?

The leader skill requires at least one additional unit from the same category on the field. Deploying Snowdin Dust or another Dust-category unit during the match satisfies the condition; the skill will not trigger passively on its own.

### Can you buy Corridor Dust Sans directly with Robux?

The native game page does not list a direct Robux purchase price for this unit. Access relies on repeatedly clearing General Corridor stages until the drop item appears, so plan for a grind rather than a store purchase.

### Is Corridor Dust Sans good in Infinite mode?

Yes — that is where he performs best. His stacking +10% damage passive has no observed cap in community testing, so longer waves give him time to ramp into genuinely high critical damage. Just make sure his Dust-category partner is deployed first.
