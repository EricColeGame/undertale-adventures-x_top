## Traits and Leveling: Turning Good Units Into Carries

Two systems do the heavy lifting in Undertale Adventures X: leveling and traits. Leveling raises a unit's baseline performance, which makes every other investment scale better. Traits are rolled bonuses that can push a unit well past its default output — or waste your resources if you roll carelessly.

For a consolidated reference on units, traits, and raid content, bookmark the [Undertale Adventures X wiki reference page](https://undertaleadventuresx.wiki/) and use it alongside your own testing.

| Investment | What it changes | Priority for new players |
|---|---|---|
| Unit levels | Baseline stats and consistency | Highest — level what you actually field |
| Trait rolls | Bonus stats on top of levels | High — start with your main damage dealer |
| New units | Roster options and role coverage | Medium — fill gaps, do not collect dust |
| Raids farming | Token income for progression | Medium-high — steady passive growth |
| Gamepass boosts | Extra yield from Raids mode | Optional — only if you farm daily |

A simple trait-rolling routine keeps you from spreading thin:

1. Pick one carry and level it as far as your resources allow.
2. Roll traits on that unit until you land something meaningfully better.
3. Stop rerolling once a good trait lands — chasing perfection costs more than it returns.
4. Move to the next unit and repeat, working from damage outward.
5. Re-test an old stage to confirm the upgrade actually changed your clear speed.

Players who level five units to a mediocre point usually lose to players who level one unit properly and let it carry the run. Depth beats breadth early.

## Raids, Tokens, and the AFK Chamber: Progression That Keeps Running

Raids mode is where long-term progression lives in Undertale Adventures X. Clearing raids earns Tokens, and Tokens fund the roster growth that makes harder stages possible. Because raid rewards scale with what you can consistently finish, the smart play is to farm the highest raid you clear reliably rather than gambling on one you barely survive.

| Progression source | What you get | Best practice |
|---|---|---|
| Raids mode | Tokens for roster progression | Farm the hardest raid you clear consistently |
| AFK Chamber | Passive resources while you are away | Queue it before you log off |
| Codes | Free rewards when a code is active | Redeem as soon as you spot a new one |
| +50% Tokens gamepass | Higher Token yield from Raids | Worth it only if you raid regularly |

The AFK Chamber is the most underrated system for busy players. It accumulates resources without you running active stages, which means your account keeps moving forward during school, work, or sleep. Combine that passive income with a few focused raid clears per session, and your roster improves without marathon grinding.

The Token gamepass follows the same logic. A 50% boost to Raid Tokens compounds over dozens of runs, so it pays off for dedicated farmers and does very little for someone who raids twice a week.
