## How to Prepare for Undertale Adventures X Raid Act 1

Prep is where most clears are actually won. Before you queue, three systems matter: Souls (the summoning currency), trait rerolls (your stat upgrades), and codes (free injections of both).

### Souls and summoning

Soul Fragments combine into Souls, and Souls are what you spend to summon units. Arriving at a raid with an empty Soul bank means you have no way to pivot if your first composition fails. Player experience consistently suggests banking enough for at least one or two emergency summons before you start a serious attempt.

### Trait rerolls

Rerolls are spent in the upgrades area to buff your team's stats. The most common mistake here is spreading them thin across the whole roster. Concentrate them on the two or three units that will actually anchor your raid lane — a single heavily upgraded carry outperforms six mediocre ones.

## Best Unit Roles for Undertale Adventures X Raid Act 1

Raid compositions are less about collecting every recognizable Undertale character and more about covering jobs. A team that covers all five roles below will usually outperform a team of five high-rarity damage dealers.

| Role | Job in the raid | What to prioritize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lane anchor / DPS | Holds the main path | High single-target damage and solid range |
| AoE clear | Deletes grouped waves | Wide splash and fast attack speed |
| Support / buffer | Lifts nearby units | Damage or speed buffs, cheap to place |
| Economy / farm | Generates currency mid-run | Early placement in a safe back-line spot |
| Boss finisher | Bursts the final threat | High burst damage and a short cooldown |

A reliable starting shape is one economy unit, two anchors, one AoE unit, and one support or finisher. If your roster is thin, cut the support first — economy and anchors are non-negotiable, because they're what let you keep scaling as waves get heavier.

### Placement beats rarity

Where a unit sits matters more than how rare it is. Anchors should cover the longest stretch of path available, AoE units should sit where enemies bunch up, and economy units belong at the back where nothing reaches them. Spend the first few waves watching how enemies move before you commit your layout.

## Undertale Adventures X Raid Act 1 Strategy and Common Mistakes

The core raid loop is simple: place a minimal opener, upgrade before you expand, and re-balance lanes whenever damage is landing where enemies aren't. Most failed attempts come down to one of six habits.

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placing everything at the start | You run dry and can't afford upgrades | Open small, upgrade, then expand |
| Spreading rerolls across the roster | No unit becomes strong enough to matter | Stack rerolls on 2–3 core units |
| Skipping economy units | Late waves simply overwhelm you | Slot one farm unit in a safe spot |
| Upgrading the wrong lane | Damage lands where nothing walks | Re-check the path every few waves |
| Queuing with an empty Soul bank | Zero flexibility after a failed attempt | Bank Souls before starting |
| Redeeming codes too late | Free rewards expire unused | Redeem immediately when a code is live |

### Ability timing and the final push

Save your biggest abilities for the closing waves rather than firing them the moment they're ready. The last stretch of a raid is usually where the boss-style threat appears, and an ability that's already on cooldown when it arrives is an ability you wasted. If you're unsure, hold one burst ability in reserve specifically for the finish.

## FAQ

### Do I need a full team to clear Undertale Adventures X Raid Act 1?

No, but you need a balanced one. Five units covering economy, anchoring, AoE, and burst will clear far more reliably than a stacked roster of pure damage dealers. If you're short on units, prioritize economy and lane anchors.

### How many units should I place before upgrading?

Open with as few as your economy allows, then upgrade before adding more. Raid waves scale faster than early placements do, so an upgraded core usually beats a wide, weak spread.

### Are codes worth using for raid prep?

Yes — they're free Souls and rerolls with no downside. Redeem every working code before you queue, and check a maintained list regularly, since some codes expire within hours of release.

### What should I do after clearing Raid Act 1?

Move into the next raid act while your team is tuned, and only re-farm Act 1 when you need a specific reward. Keep banking Souls and rerolls so you're never entering a raid with an empty reserve.
