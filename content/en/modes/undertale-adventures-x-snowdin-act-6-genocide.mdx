## Best Units and Team Composition for Snowdin Act 6 Genocide

The single biggest mistake players make on the Undertale Adventures X Snowdin Act 6 Genocide route is bringing a normal-mode team. Normal mode lets you brute-force with one over-leveled carry. Genocide does not, because the waves split your attention between long lanes and dense enemy clusters.

Build around five roles instead of one star:

| Role | What to Look For | Why It Wins Here |
|---|---|---|
| Area damage | Multi-target attacks with decent range | Clears the elite clusters that stall normal teams |
| Single-target burst | High damage against one target | The boss phase is a damage check, not a crowd test |
| Support / buffer | Damage or attack-speed buffs | Multiplies your whole lineup instead of one unit |
| Staller / debuffer | Slow, stun, or repositioning effects | Buys seconds, and seconds are the whole game |
| Cheap filler | Low-cost units with reliable uptime | Covers the early waves so your carries stay in reserve |

Placement philosophy matters as much as the roster. Put your cheapest units at the first bend to soften waves, keep your burst unit near the final stretch so it's always in range of the boss, and leave one slot free for a panic placement when a leak sneaks through.

Trait rerolls are the quiet MVP of this mode. A mediocre unit with a strong trait will outperform a good unit with a bad one, so spend rerolls on your two main damage dealers before you touch anything else. Community reports consistently point to that order of operations as the cheapest way to close the gap between a failed Genocide attempt and a clean one.

## Wave-by-Wave Strategy for Snowdin Act 6 Genocide

Genocide waves tend to follow a rhythm, and knowing the rhythm is what turns a chaotic run into a plan.

| Phase | What Happens | What You Should Be Doing |
|---|---|---|
| Opening waves | Light, spread-out enemies | Place cheap units early, bank currency, don't over-invest |
| Mid waves | Denser packs, first armored units | Upgrade your area damage; start cycling cooldowns |
| Elite waves | Tougher enemies with support behind them | Hold one burst ability in reserve for the spike |
| Pre-boss | Sustained pressure with fewer breaks | Stop upgrading filler; funnel everything into carries |
| Boss | Single high-durability target with phases | Stack buffs before the phase change, not during it |

Three habits separate clears from wipes:

- **Watch the track, not the health bars.** Positioning errors kill runs faster than damage shortfalls.
- **Bank one ability.** The pre-boss wave is designed to bait out your cooldowns so the boss walks through an empty board.
- **Restart early.** If you leak in the opening waves, the run is already over. Restarting at wave three saves twenty minutes.

If you're farming rather than clearing for the first time, the same route becomes much more relaxed once your roster is leveled — the difficulty curve flattens hard after your first successful clear.

## Pro Tips and Common Mistakes

Even strong rosters fail this mode for avoidable reasons. The five most common:

1. **Treating it like normal mode.** The same team that walked through Act 6 normally will stall on the first elite wave here.
2. **Ignoring trait rerolls.** Traits are a free stat multiplier sitting unused in your inventory.
3. **Over-investing in early waves.** Currency spent on filler is currency your carries never get.
4. **Placing everything at the entrance.** Later placement means more total damage per unit across the whole path.
5. **Skipping support units.** A buffer that increases team damage by a flat percentage is worth more than a third damage dealer in a long fight.

One more: check your codes before a serious attempt. Free Souls and rerolls cost you nothing and directly raise your odds. Expired codes are worthless, so redeem them the moment you find them.

## FAQ

**Is Snowdin Act 6 Genocide required to progress in Undertale Adventures X?**
No — it's an optional hard mode. Normal Act 6 clears the chapter. Genocide is for players chasing better rewards, faster Soul farming, and the challenge itself.

**What's the minimum team I need for the Undertale Adventures X Snowdin Act 6 Genocide clear?**
Community reports point to at least one strong area-damage unit, one single-target burst unit, and one support unit as the practical floor. You can clear with less, but your placement has to be flawless.

**Do promo codes help with Genocide mode?**
Indirectly, yes. Codes grant Souls, rerolls, and upgrade materials, all of which raise your team's power level without any in-run cost. They don't change the mode itself.

**How long does a Genocide attempt take?**
Longer than a normal clear, mostly because enemies take more hits and failed runs eat time. Once your roster is leveled and you know the wave timings, a clean attempt is roughly comparable to a normal Act 6 run.

**Should I farm Genocide or normal mode for Souls?**
Normal mode is faster per run, but Genocide pays more per completion. If your team can clear Genocide reliably, it's the better farm. If it can't, grind normal until it can.
