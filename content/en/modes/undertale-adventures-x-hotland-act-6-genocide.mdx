In Undertale Adventures X, Hotland, Act 6, and Genocide are three separate levers that change what a run pays out, and mixing them up costs more time than any bad summon. ## Act 6, Genocide, and the "All Acts Pay the Same" Myth

One of the most repeated lines in older guides is that every act pays the same XP and souls. That was accurate in a narrow window: an August 3, 2024 prep video said every act at that time paid equally, which made Act 1 Neutral the fastest XP clear and a later Genocide act the better drop farm. It stopped being reliable almost immediately afterward.

| Claim | Where it came from | Does it still hold? |
|---|---|---|
| "All acts pay the same XP and souls" | August 3, 2024 prep video | Only for that early build — do not apply it to Waterfall or later |
| Act 1 Neutral is the fastest XP clear | Same video | Reasonable for early acts, not for Act 6 |
| A later Genocide act is the drop farm | Same video | Still the general pattern players follow |
| Act 6 waves are longer, with flying enemies at the start | September 25, 2024 Act 6 Neutral footage | Community reports — expect longer runs than earlier acts |
| Trait materials stopped dropping from ordinary clears | Snowdin region notes | Yes — farm trait materials through dedicated modes |

By the time Waterfall arrived in September 2024, the economy had already shifted. Act 6 footage published on September 25, 2024 shows flying enemies opening the wave and longer waves overall than earlier acts. If you are planning an Undertale Adventures X Hotland Act 6 Genocide run today, plan for a longer sitting than an Act 1 clear, and bring answers for air units in the opening seconds of each wave.

### Why Hotland Became the Kill-Grind Spot

Hotland's reputation comes from player questions rather than patch notes: through 2025, "where do I grind kills" threads consistently pointed at Hotland and the Core beside it. The pair works because both sit late in the region order, so enemies there are worth more progress per run than Ruins or Snowdin clears.

A simple loop for the Undertale Adventures X Hotland Act 6 Genocide grind:

- Clear the Act 6 opening with splash or multi-target units, since flying enemies arrive first.
- Bank souls and XP on a repeatable stage rather than a one-time story clear.
- Move to Hotland or the Core once your units can survive the longer waves.
- Spend trait rerolls in batches rather than one at a time — the trait pool has 12 entries.

## FAQ

**Is Undertale Adventures X free to play?**
Yes. It is a free Roblox tower defense experience by Side Productions. You defend a base with Undertale-inspired units, level those units, and strengthen them with traits. There is no paid entry requirement to start.

**What is the difference between Act 6 and Genocide in Undertale Adventures X?**
Act 6 is a campaign act — September 2024 footage shows longer waves with flying enemies up front. Genocide is a run type, and a Genocide act is traditionally the drop farm rather than the fast XP clear. Most players combine the two when planning an Undertale Adventures X Hotland Act 6 Genocide session.

**Where should I grind kills in Undertale Adventures X?**
Community reports point to Hotland and the Core, the areas players named next to each other when asking where to farm kills in 2025. Both sit late in the region order, so each run is worth more than an early Ruins or Snowdin clear.

**Do the old trait rates still apply?**
Partly. Trait rerolls assign one of 12 listed traits, and the circulating rate table matches an August 2024 odds screen for Royal Guard Soul at 15%. Check your own Traits menu before committing a large stack of rerolls, because the table you found may be older than the build you are playing.
