## How Evolution Works in Undertale Adventures X

Undertale Adventures X is a Roblox tower defense game built around recognizable Undertale characters, and your units are your most important asset. You summon them with Souls, which are crafted by combining Soul Fragments, and you strengthen them through a few separate systems that players often blur together: leveling, evolving, and trait rerolling.

Evolution is the permanent upgrade path. In most tower defense games of this style — and per player experience in this one — evolving a unit consumes currency and specific materials to unlock a stronger form. That new form usually comes with higher base stats, better scaling as you level it again, and sometimes an upgraded or reworked ability.

The exact requirements live inside each unit's evolution panel, and they can change between updates. Always read that panel before you start spending. A unit that looks cheap to evolve at low levels may need rare materials once you reach the final tier.

### Evolution vs. leveling vs. traits

| System | Main cost | What it changes | Can it be undone? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leveling | Souls and XP from clearing chapters | Raw stats within the unit's current form | No, but it carries into the evolved form |
| Evolution | Souls plus unit-specific materials | Base stats, scaling, sometimes a new ability | No — treat it as permanent |
| Trait rerolls | Trait Rerolls (earned or redeemed) | Bonus stats such as damage, range, or cooldown | Yes — rerolls are repeatable |

The takeaway: leveling is temporary power, evolution is permanent power, and traits are the fine-tuning layer you apply on top.

## Evolution Resources and Where to Farm Them

Before you chase evolution, understand the economy. Community reports consistently point to Souls as the bottleneck, because the same currency that summons new units also feeds your upgrades. Spending everything on summons is the fastest way to stall your progression.

| Resource | What it is used for | Best source |
|---|---|---|
| Souls | Summoning units and paying evolution costs | Combining Soul Fragments; chapter clears; codes |
| Soul Fragments | Crafted into Souls | Regular stage grinding and event rewards |
| Trait Rerolls | Rerolling a unit's bonus trait | Upgrades area purchases and codes |
| Gaster Hands | Summon-related item handed out by codes | Promotional codes |
| Unit materials | Required by higher evolution tiers | The unit's evolution panel lists the exact source |

Two habits separate efficient players from stuck ones. First, bank a reserve of Souls before a new banner or update drops. Second, only reroll traits on units you have already committed to evolving — rerolls spent on a placeholder unit are rerolls wasted.

## Step-by-Step: Evolving a Unit Without Wasting Souls

The order you do things in matters more than raw playtime. Follow this sequence and you will rarely waste a resource.

| Step | Action | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick one carry unit and commit to it | Spreading Souls across six units leaves all six mediocre |
| 2 | Level the unit to the cap required by its evolution panel | Evolution is often gated behind a level requirement |
| 3 | Farm Soul Fragments and convert them into Souls | Fragments are the reliable, repeatable income stream |
| 4 | Collect the listed materials before you evolve | Starting an evolution you cannot finish locks up your resources |
| 5 | Confirm the evolution in the unit menu | The new form resets your leveling curve, so expect to re-level |
| 6 | Reroll traits after evolving, not before | Traits apply to the final form, so rerolling early wastes rolls |
| 7 | Re-test the chapter that was blocking you | Evolution should turn a wall into a clear |

A quick note on step 6: many players reroll a trait on a base unit, evolve it, and then discover the trait no longer fits the evolved ability. Do the evolution first, then chase the trait you actually want.

## Evolution Priorities: Which Units Deserve Your Souls

Not every unit is worth evolving, and the ones that are depend on your roster. Still, the priority logic is consistent across tower defense games like this one.

| Role | Evolve first? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Main damage dealer | Yes — top priority | A single evolved carry clears chapters that a whole team of base units cannot |
| Area-of-effect damage | Yes — second | Wave-clear speed is what gates most chapters |
| Support or buffer | Later | Buffs scale with the units they support, so evolve the supported unit first |
| Staller or slower | Later | Utility units function fine at low investment for a long time |
| Economy or farm unit | Situational | Only worth it if the evolved form meaningfully increases payout |

### Common evolution mistakes to avoid

- **Evolving a unit you never use** just because the materials were available.
- **Rerolling traits before evolving**, which wastes Trait Rerolls on a temporary form.
- **Skipping the level requirement check**, then discovering you are short of Souls mid-evolution.
- **Ignoring codes**, which are free Souls and rerolls that cost nothing but a few seconds.
- **Abandoning a half-built carry** to chase a shiny new summon from the latest banner.

## Frequently Asked Questions About This Undertale Adventures X Evolution Guide

**What is the fastest way to farm Souls in Undertale Adventures X?**

Combine every Soul Fragment you collect, replay the highest chapter you can clear reliably, and redeem codes the moment they go live. Codes are the single fastest injection of Souls, because they cost nothing but the ten seconds it takes to type them in.

**Do I need duplicate units to evolve a unit in Undertale Adventures X?**

Requirements vary by unit and by evolution tier, and they change between updates. Some evolutions ask for materials rather than duplicates. Check the unit's own evolution panel — it lists exactly what that specific unit needs, so you never have to guess.

**Should I reroll traits before or after evolving?**

After. Traits are applied to the form you are using, so rerolling a base unit and then evolving it usually wastes the roll. Evolve first, confirm the new ability, then spend Trait Rerolls to match that ability.

**Will this Undertale Adventures X evolution guide stay accurate after updates?**

The core loop — farm Souls, evolve one carry at a time, reroll traits afterward — is stable and unlikely to change. Specific costs, materials, and code rewards are the parts that shift, so treat the tables here as a framework and confirm the numbers in-game before you commit resources.

Stick to the sequence, protect your Soul reserve, and evolve one unit at a time. That approach will carry you through far more chapters than a scattered roster ever will.
